Margin (m-3)

m-3 → margin on all sides = 1rem

Margin Top (mt-4)

mt-4 → margin-top = 1.5rem

Padding X & Y (px-4, py-2)

px-4 → padding-left & right = 1.5rem
py-2 → padding-top & bottom = 0.5rem

Padding Start & End (ps-5, pe-2)

ps-5 → padding-left = 3rem
pe-2 → padding-right = 0.5rem

Margin Auto (mx-auto)

mx-auto → margin-left & margin-right = auto (Centers this column)
